## 30.4 EDITOR -- a desktop app beside the game

The user: *"Make a new program modifying areas, building hitboxes and loot spawns. container spawns and also sprites for houses, vehicles, mobs and containers."* -- a desktop app beside the game -- and *"the editor app should allow for tile editing and zombie spawn type editing as well. - **Five main functions**, each a screen:
  1. **Edit interest points** -- the kinds of place (`data/places.lua`, `data/maps/prefabs/`, the fills): a place's layout (its prefab or its fill rules), its ground **tiles** (paint the ground and road cells of a prefab or a place's area), its **zombie spawn types** (the archetype weights per place, its spawn points), and where it may stand (seasons, the user's "bases later in the world").
  2. **Edit buildings** -- per building (`data/buildings.lua`, `data/colliders.lua`, `data/sprite_anchors.lua`): the facade and the interior plate drawn together, the **hitboxes** (footprint rects and the room's walls) dragged and resized, the door's point and leaf, the **loot spots** (container pieces placed in the room and the table each draws from), and its **sprites** (which facade and plate it uses).

  4. **Edit mobs** -- the infected and the animals (`data/zombies.lua`, `data/animals.lua`): each kind's sprite set, its numbers, and where it spawns (the user's "military only in bases").

- **Saving** writes the game's own data files, in place, **keeping their comments and layout**: edit the one table entry that changed (find its span in the source and replace that text), never regenerate a whole hand-written file. After a save the editor runs the game's lints (`python tools/verify.py --subject lint`) and shows what they say. An undo, and a "changed, not saved" mark.
- **Preview**: what is edited is drawn as the game draws it (the atlas, the anchors, the colliders as F1 draws them); a "play it" button launches the game (`run.bat --world <seed>`) to try the change.
- **Build it in steps, each committed and usable**: (1) the app shell, the data loading, the safe in-place writer and **Edit buildings** (hitboxes, doors, loot spots, sprites); (2) **Edit objects**, **Edit mobs** and **Edit loot tables**; (3) **Edit interest points** with the tiles and the zombie spawn types (after 30.3 merges, since it changes their data). This track is step (1); steps (2) and (3) follow as their own tracks.
- Scenarios: the editor is a tool, so test its writer, not its clicks: a lint-style check (Python or a headless LÖVE run of the editor's modules) that editing one entry of each data file and saving changes exactly that entry's text and leaves the rest byte for byte, and that the game still loads and passes its lints after a save.
